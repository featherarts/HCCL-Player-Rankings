HCCL Scorecard PDF Update + Auto Add Players Guide
================================

This feature updates your HCCL Stats CSV from one STUMPS scorecard PDF.

Before using it, add a final column to your stats CSV:

Stumps Name / Stumps Name / Scorecard Username

Put the exact scorecard name for each player, for example:
- Sasi18
- Pasindu Dilshan
- Kalana Thenu
- Wenura Sandeepa

You can also write multiple aliases separated by commas:
Kalana, Kalana Thenu

Weekly process:
1. Upload latest HCCL Stats.csv in the sidebar.
2. Open Scorecard Update tab.
3. Upload the new match PDF.
4. Review parsed batting/bowling tables.
5. Download updated stats CSV.
6. Upload downloaded CSV in sidebar.
7. Check rankings.
8. Save snapshot to Supabase.

Important notes:
- The app expects STUMPS scorecard PDF format.
- If any names are unmatched, fix Stumps Name / Stumps Name / Scorecard Username and try again.
- The app does not overwrite your original CSV.
- Keep a backup of each weekly stats CSV.


## v4.2 Auto-add players

This version supports the user's new CSV layout with `Stumps Name` after `NAME`.

If a player appears in a STUMPS scorecard PDF but is not found in the CSV by `Stumps Name`, `Scorecard Username`, or `NAME`, the app automatically adds a new player row to the downloaded updated stats CSV. The new row starts with zero career stats and then applies the match stats from the PDF.

Recommended weekly process:
1. Upload your latest HCCL Stats CSV.
2. Open the Scorecard Update tab.
3. Upload the match scorecard PDF.
4. Review matched players and new players added.
5. Download the updated HCCL Stats CSV.
6. Upload the downloaded CSV in the sidebar to generate updated rankings.
